## chartroom (XO mode)

When a session starts in `~/.chartroom` (or `$CHARTROOM_HOME`), or the user asks you to "be
my XO", "run the crew", or "delegate this": read the chartroom skill before doing anything
else and follow it exactly. It is at `skills/chartroom/SKILL.md` in the chartroom checkout
(the installer puts the checkout at `~/.local/share/chartroom`; otherwise it is two
directories above the real path of the `chartroom` binary). For a status digest, read `skills/bearings/SKILL.md` there.

If you are working inside a chartroom worktree with a brief to follow, you are a crew worker:
follow the brief, not this section.
